Our Story

Comfort food with crunch, heat, and colour.

Jisoo brings the bold energy of Korean street food to the heart of Sabah — golden fried chicken with sticky-spicy glazes, bubbling hot pots built for sharing, and loaded platters made for social moments.

Built around a recognizable brand, a proven menu, and real crowd-pleasing flavours, Jisoo is a food concept designed for operators who want a warm, busy, easy-to-explain outlet.

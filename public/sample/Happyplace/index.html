Our Story

happy place

Nestled in Kolombong, Happy Place Restaurant is where an industrial chic aesthetic meets botanical warmth. With exposed structural beams, rustic brickwork, and suspended greenery, the ambiance is purposefully designed to feel spacious yet intimately welcoming.

We serve hearty, modern Western cuisine crafted to bring joy to every table. Whether you're gathering for a casual meal, a family feast, or a special celebration, this is your escape from the everyday.

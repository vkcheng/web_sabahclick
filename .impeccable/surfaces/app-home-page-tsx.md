## Scope

Home page (`/`), Persuade mode. Replaces the "Obsidian" world entirely (user request 2026-11-24: full visual redesign after the business pivot away from web design).

## Audience, job, action

Sabah business owners, managers and teams (mostly Chinese-reading, site defaults to English, EN/中文/BM). They must understand the three offers (AI education, AI in daily operations, custom software), see their own workday in the page, and send us the jobs they want fixed. Primary action: WhatsApp message pre-filled with the steps the visitor armed. Contact form stays as the secondary channel.

Proof on hand: VKscanner only. Sample workdays are synthetic demonstration content and labeled as samples; no invented clients, numbers or testimonials.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a rhythm-machine step row of a business's workday: sixteen jobs from opening to closing, and the visitor programs which ones AI should take. It refuses the AI-agency default of headline, three service cards and a robot glow.

OWN-WORLD: Matte charcoal panel (#1A1A1A) with silkscreen-grey labels; the step row quartered red, orange, yellow, white by time of day (morning, midday, afternoon, closing); lit LEDs and a red seven-segment readout are the only glow. Condensed industrial caps (Saira Condensed) for display and silkscreen labels; system CJK sans for Chinese. Controls are hardware keys and square buttons, never rounded cards.

STORY: Visitor sees their own day as sixteen keys, taps the repetitive jobs, sees which of our three services each one routes to (LEARN, ASSIST, BUILD), then sends that list to us on WhatsApp. Below, each service is a panel module, then how we start (meet in person, one workflow first, expand), VKscanner as our shipped proof, and contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with wordmark, silkscreen nav, language keys and a WhatsApp button. Left: two-line display headline and one paragraph. Right: a control panel with clock readout (time of the chase step), pattern selector (sample business type), RUN/STOP and CLEAR, armed count, and three route LEDs with counts. Full-width beneath: the sixteen-key step row with bank labels, a detail strip for the focused step, and the primary action "Send my N steps on WhatsApp" at the row's right end. Mobile: the row becomes two rows of eight.

FORM: Challenger world, catalog signals-instruments-drum-machine-step-row, chosen by the user over the assigned Gaya Street market direction. seed:bbf91f679b474b66. Signature interaction: tap arms a step, second tap accents it, the chase light sweeps the day in tempo and the readout tracks its clock time; route LEDs and service modules light for armed steps. Motion grammar: chase light and key press flash only, locked to one clock; reduced motion stops the chase and keeps instant state changes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

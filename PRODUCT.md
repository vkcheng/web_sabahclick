## Platform

web

## Users

- **Business owners and managers in Kota Kinabalu and across Sabah** who want to understand where AI is worth using in their business, and then get it into daily operations.
- **Company staff teams** sent by their employer for training: they need to come away able to use AI tools in their own everyday work.
- **Individuals** who sign up for public AI courses and workshops on their own.
- **Businesses that need software built**: an owner or manager with a specific operational problem (orders, bookings, stock, customer records, reports, scattered tools) who wants a system made for it.

## Product Purpose

**Framing on the site (updated 2026-11-24 by VK):** the core idea is *using AI to help local businesses with their daily workflows*. The home page leads with that idea, then presents custom builds (web systems, mobile apps, daily workflow systems, with AI built in where it helps), and AI efficiency training last. Visitors should not be asked to do work (picking tasks) before they understand the company; the contact form lets them multi-select the services they need and the problems they face, then sends that to WhatsApp. "Web systems" means custom browser-based systems (booking, ordering, portals, dashboards), not marketing-site web design.

SabahClick is a Kota Kinabalu company with three lines of work (confirmed 2026-11-24):

1. **AI education**: courses, workshops, and in-company training on using AI, for owners and management, staff teams, and individuals.
2. **AI in daily operations**: working with a business to bring AI into the tasks its team repeats every day.
3. **Custom software**: building systems for a client's specific needs.

Success is a business whose people actually use AI and the systems built for them in day-to-day work, not a one-off talk or a shelved tool.

SabahClick **no longer offers web design** (as of 2026-11-24). Web design, website portfolios, local SEO, and web-design pricing must not appear on the site.

## Positioning

- **Local and face to face.** Based in Kota Kinabalu; clients can meet in person, and SabahClick can come to see how the business actually runs.
- **Teaching and building under one roof.** The same team that teaches a company's people to use AI can also build the system they need, so clients don't have to choose between a trainer who can't build and a developer who can't teach.
- **Start small.** Begin with one workflow or one team, prove it helps, then expand. No big packaged bundles up front.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Custom software SabahClick builds:
- Internal management systems: stock, orders, bookings, customer records, reports.
- Mobile apps (Android / iOS).
- AI assistants and automation: WhatsApp customer-service assistants, document recognition, automatic reports.
- Integration: connecting a client's existing tools so data flows between them.

Own product: **VKscanner**, a free Android document scanner with OCR and PDF export that runs fully on-device and offline, with no sign-in, ads or analytics (`/apps`, privacy policy at `/vkscanner/privacy`). It stays on the site as SabahClick's own shipped work.

Undecided (do not invent): course names, schedules, durations, prices, class sizes, certificates; software pricing; delivery timelines.

## Product Principles

1. **Learn, then use.** Every offer ends in people doing their real work with AI, not in a slide deck.
2. **One workflow first.** Propose the smallest useful starting point; expansion is earned.
3. **Show up in person.** Being local and reachable is part of the product, not a footnote.
4. **Plain words.** Owners and staff who are new to AI should understand every line; no jargon or terminal costume.
5. **Honest claims only.** Describe what we do and how; no invented proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience includes non-technical owners and staff, many reading in Chinese: keep copy plain, legible at comfortable sizes, and complete in all three languages.
